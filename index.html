Ampere Shows Which Chargers Fit Your Car And Range, Then A Host Confirms Before You Set Off.

Public and Private Chargers, Finally in One Search

Ampere brings public EV charger discovery and private charger sharing into one web app for India, so drivers search once, compare access and price, and request a confirmed slot.

Charging takes more than finding a pin on a map. Ampere checks the connector, the range, the access and the full price before a driver sets off.

Built Around How Drivers Really Charge

Five connected parts that take a driver from search to a confirmed charging session.

Fewer Searches, Smarter Trips

Ampere combines five checks so drivers make fewer disconnected searches and fewer avoidable trips to unsuitable chargers.

How Ampere Works

Search once with your car, battery and destination. Ampere hides chargers that do not fit, holds the slot while a host responds, and shows each stage until charging is done.
